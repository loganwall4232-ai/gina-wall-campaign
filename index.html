Get to Know Gina

District 24

I have proudly called Alaska home for the past 35 years, including the last 24 years in Eagle River/Chugiak, where my husband and I have raised our family, planting strong roots and creating meaningful ties to the community. Through my work and life experience, I stand by the value of consistency, accountability, and showing up for people. I believe effective leadership requires the courage to make tough decisions, stay grounded in common sense, and remain focused on building a better community.

Gina Wall was born in Fairbanks into a family with a tradition of military service. The military way of life took her to many parts of the world and inspired a deep love of travel. Her family eventually settled in Alaska permanently in the mid-1990s, and Gina graduated from Palmer High School.

After graduation, Gina moved to Anchorage and attended the University of Alaska Anchorage, where she earned both her bachelor’s and master’s degrees, along with a post-graduate certificate.

My Vision for House District 24

Steady and decisive leadership focused on results, listening to our community, thoughtfully processing details, and having the courage to make the decisions needed to move our state and district forward.

Family & Career

Twenty-four years in Eagle River and Chugiak

Gina and her husband, Doug, a longtime resident of Eagle River and proud Chugiak Mustang graduate, have raised their two sons, Jackson and Logan, in the Eagle River/Chugiak community for the past 24 years. Doug and Gina were married at Peace Lutheran Church, where they remain members.

Over the course of the past 24 years, Gina has worked to support others through some of life’s most difficult challenges. From serving as a domestic violence legal advocate during college, to working as a substance abuse counselor helping women navigate the complexities of addiction, to her current role as a high school counselor and Licensed Professional Counselor (LPC), Gina has developed a deep passion for helping others find stability, healing, and growth.

Community & Life

Community and common sense

Gina believes in community and common sense. Her priorities are focused on providing opportunities for strong education and ensuring our kids have access to experiences and resources that support healthy development. She is committed to helping young people grow academically, socially, and emotionally in a supportive environment.

Gina also believes in balance in life and the importance of working hard while finding joy in everyday moments. She loves to travel, read, and craft, and she values time spent with family. She is also developing her pickleball skills, enjoys fishing when the salmon are running, and spending time drifting the upper Kenai.

Character & Commitment

Steady, decisive, and willing to have hard conversations

Gina is steady, decisive, skilled at navigating complex issues, willing to have hard conversations, and deeply committed to serving her community for the common good.

Her years as a counselor, advocate, and community member have given her the tools to listen deeply, think carefully, and act with purpose, exactly the qualities District 24 needs in its next representative.

Focused Priorities

My Vision In Action

Grounded in community values and common sense, because District 24 deserves a representative who delivers.

Education for Every Child

Every child in Eagle River and Chugiak deserves access to a quality education and the resources to thrive. I am committed to supporting schools, teachers, and the programs that give our kids the tools they need to succeed academically, socially, and emotionally.

Public Safety & Family Wellness

Safe communities are the foundation of a thriving district. I will advocate for adequate funding for public safety while also addressing the mental health and family wellness needs that keep our neighborhoods strong and our families supported.

Responsible Spending & Strong Local Economy

Alaska’s future depends on a strong and diversified economy that responsibly supports development, encourages local business growth, and creates lasting opportunities for families. The families of Eagle River and Chugiak work hard for what they earn, and I will work to support responsible government, strong economic opportunities, and policies that help hardworking families succeed.

Responsive, Courageous Leadership

Our community deserves a representative who shows up, listens carefully, and has the courage to make tough decisions. I will be accessible, transparent, and unwavering in my commitment to the people of House District 24.
